# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Techtronica project to be a respectful place to report problems, ask questions, and contribute improvements. Participation is open to people regardless of experience, background, identity, or contribution size.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Describe disagreements with evidence and focus on the work.
- Accept feedback and corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, private member records, secrets, or screenshots containing them.
- Follow the project's security reporting process for vulnerabilities instead of publishing exploit details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, repeated disruptive conduct, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/tarunchaudharydev). Do not use a public issue for private or sensitive reports.

The maintainer will review reports as available and may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. The project currently has no formal response-time commitment or separate enforcement committee. Reports will be handled with discretion, while preserving information needed to respond fairly.

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related public discussions.
